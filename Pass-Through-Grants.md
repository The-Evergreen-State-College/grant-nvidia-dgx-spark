### 1. Grants That Explicitly Allow or Require University Foundation Pass-Through

| Funder | Typical Award | Foundation Eligibility & Rules | Cycle / Timing | Best Framing for DGX Spark |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Employees Community Fund (ECF) of Boeing Puget Sound** | **Up to $50,000** (*Supporting Grants*) | Explicitly funds 501(c)(3) nonprofits in Thurston/Puget Sound for **equipment & technology upgrades**. Request must be ≤10% of applicant's gross annual revenue (easily met by Evergreen Foundation). No 1:1 match required. | Quarterly / semi-annual windows via Boeing Puget Sound ECF portal | Workforce readiness & hands-on AI/data-science lab hardware for Puget Sound students |
| **The Norcliffe Foundation** (Seattle, WA) | **$10,000 – $75,000+** | Funds capital and **equipment grants** for Western Washington 501(c)(3) educational and scientific organizations, including college foundations. No $100K floor. | **Rolling year-round** (Board reviews every ~6–8 weeks) | Expanding applied STEM & scientific computing capacity in the South Sound |
| **The Boeing Company** (Corporate Citizenship / Global Engagement) | **$15,000 – $100,000+** | Separate from ECF; corporate policy bars direct grants to state universities and **requires applying through the university's 501(c)(3) Foundation**. | Annual / invitation-aligned via Corporate & Foundation Relations | Post-secondary AI literacy, aerospace/engineering data workflows, and diverse STEM pipelines |
| **U.S. Bank Foundation** (*Community Possible – Work* Pillar) | **$10,000 – $90,000** | Explicitly awards workforce/technical equipment grants to **Washington 501(c)(3) college foundations** (e.g., $90K to Clark College Foundation for lab training equipment). | Rolling / regional invitation in Western WA | Career-connected technical and AI workflow training for first-generation & low-income students |
| **KeyBank Foundation** | **$15,000 – $100,000+** | Channels higher-education workforce grants directly through **501(c)(3) college foundations** in the Olympia–Tacoma corridor. | Rolling / regional review | Applied computational and AI skills aligned with regional employer demand |
| **Community Foundation of South Puget Sound** (Olympia, WA) | **$1,500 – $7,500** (Competitive)<br>**Any amount** (DAF) | Based in Olympia; funds 501(c)(3) organizations serving Thurston, Mason, and Lewis counties, and facilitates Donor-Advised Fund (DAF) pass-throughs to Evergreen Foundation. | Competitive cycle opens **January 2027**; DAF grants are year-round | Single DGX Spark node (~$5K–$7K) supporting community-engaged environmental/data projects |
| **Puget Sound Energy (PSE) Foundation** | **$5,000 – $25,000** | Regularly grants directly to **Washington 501(c)(3) college foundations** for STEM training, environmental stewardship, and sustainability initiatives. | Annual competitive & partnership cycles | Local, energy-efficient AI compute for environmental, climate, and watershed modeling |
