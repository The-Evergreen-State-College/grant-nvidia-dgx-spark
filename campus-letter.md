**Subject:** Collaborative Grant Opportunity ($32,000): Local AI & HPC Compute for STEM Curriculum (NVIDIA DGX Spark Developer Kit)

Dear Colleagues,

I am writing to invite collaboration across **Academic Affairs**, **The Evergreen State College Foundation / Advancement**, and the **Evergreen Grants and Contracts Office** on a **$32,000** external equipment grant initiative to bring desktop supercomputing to the **Computer Applications Lab for Scientific Computing**.

### Project Overview

As artificial intelligence, GPU-accelerated simulation, and large-scale data analysis become core competencies across the sciences, relying on commercial cloud GPU rentals or third-party APIs creates recurring per-token costs and raises data-privacy concerns for unpublished environmental, biological, and community datasets.

To give Evergreen students equitable, hands-on access to production-grade compute without recurring cloud fees, we are seeking **$32,000** to acquire one four-node **NVIDIA DGX Spark HPC Developer Kit** (four Grace Blackwell GB10 nodes linked via a switchless 200 Gbps ConnectX-7 ring, providing **512 GB of aggregate unified memory** and **16–20 TB of local NVMe storage**). Housed on a dedicated local lab network in the **Computer Applications Lab for Scientific Computing**, the cluster will support:

* **Local Large Language Models (LLMs) & Agentic AI Workflows:** Deploying, quantizing, fine-tuning, and evaluating open-weight models locally without external API throttling or data exposure.
* **Bioacoustics & Ecological Data Science:** GPU-accelerated spectral analysis and automated species classification across multi-terabyte field recording archives.
* **Molecular Dynamics & Computational Chemistry:** Local execution of multi-node protein-ligand and materials simulations (LAMMPS, NAMD, GROMACS, OpenFold3).
* **Climate, Watershed & Geospatial Modeling:** GPU-accelerated weather, fluid dynamics, and spatial machine learning workflows focused on Puget Sound ecosystems.

Full technical specifications, an itemized **$32,000** budget (including freight and Olympia sales tax), and the draft grant summary are available in our working repository:

**[https://github.com/The-Evergreen-State-College/grant-nvidia-dgx-spark](https://github.com/The-Evergreen-State-College/grant-nvidia-dgx-spark)**
